# Human Accountability and Governance

> **AI can perform work, but a human remains accountable for using its output.**

Accepting a suggestion, merging code, approving a release, or allowing an agent to act is a human engineering decision. "The AI produced it" is not a justification.

1. **Name an owner.** Every consequential change needs someone authorized to approve it, accept remaining risk, monitor it, and stop or roll it back.
2. **Match oversight to impact.** Low-risk, reversible work can follow normal review. Security-sensitive, regulated, destructive, customer-facing, or hard-to-reverse work needs stronger and often independent review.



Do not replace evidence with model confidence.
